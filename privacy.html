Privacy Policy

FoldPlay for iPhone and iPad (com.bicreator.dualds), version 1.0
Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: FoldPlay does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. Your games, saves and settings stay on your device. If you choose to use the optional Apple Music soundtrack, Apple Music searches and plays songs under your own Apple Account; nothing is sent to us.

1. Who we are

FoldPlay is made by Manjith, an independent developer in Bengaluru, India. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer. You can reach us at manjithreddy096@gmail.com.

2. What the app works with on your device

  • Game files you import. FoldPlay opens only the game files (.nds) you choose: picked in the Files browser, opened in FoldPlay from another app, or placed in FoldPlay's own folder in the Files app (On My iPhone, FoldPlay) or in Finder on a Mac. It does not look through the rest of your files. Imported games are copied into the app's private storage; files you placed in FoldPlay's folder are then moved to its "Imported" sub-folder so they are not imported twice.
  • Details read from each game file, such as its title, icon, game code and size, to show your library and avoid duplicates.
  • Your progress: in-game saves, save states (each with a small picture of the game screen) and your library details (when you imported a game, when you last played it and for how long).
  • Settings, such as theme, screen layout, controller style, volume and display options.
  • Your soundtrack list (optional): the Apple Music songs you add to play while you game.
  • Touches and button presses, used only to control the game while you play.
  • Fold angle. On a foldable iPhone, the app reads the hinge position from iOS only to decide which screen shows the game and which shows the controls.

All of this is used only to run your games on your device. FoldPlay does not use the microphone, camera, Photos, location, contacts, Bluetooth or the local network. It asks for access to Apple Music only if you choose to connect it for the soundtrack.

3. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect personal information, game files, saves, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data. The app has no account or login, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash-reporting service and no in-app purchases. We have no server. The app makes no internet connections of its own, except the Apple Music requests described in section 6 when you use the soundtrack; those go to Apple, not to us.

The only link out of the app is "View the complete source code" in About, which opens our public source code page on GitHub in your browser. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that website.

4. Where your data is stored

Your games, saves, save states and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's storage. We cannot see them. Like other app data, they can be included in your own iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on; Apple handles those backups under the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Deleting your data

  • Delete a game from your library to remove that game together with its saves, save states and icon.
  • Delete or replace a single save state in the Save States screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored inside it.
  • Files you placed in FoldPlay's folder in the Files app (including its "Imported" sub-folder) can be deleted there at any time.
  • Remove songs from Your Soundtrack by swiping them away, and turn off Apple Music access at any time in Settings, Apps, FoldPlay.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete, correct or send to you.

6. Music while you play

  • Other music apps. By default FoldPlay lets music from other apps (such as Spotify, YouTube Music or Apple Music) keep playing while you game. FoldPlay does not see what is playing; you can turn this off in Settings.
  • Apple Music soundtrack (optional). If you connect Apple Music, FoldPlay uses Apple's MusicKit to search the Apple Music catalog and play the songs you pick. Apple handles the search, your subscription check and the streaming under your Apple Account, under the Apple Privacy Policy. FoldPlay keeps only your list of chosen songs, on your device. Playing full songs needs an Apple Music subscription.

7. Information Apple may share with us

  • Crash reports. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Share With App Developers), Apple may give us crash reports and basic usage statistics in App Store Connect. These do not identify you, and you can turn this off at any time in the same place.
  • Downloads. Apple gives developers sales and download reports (such as the date and country, without your name). We use them only to understand how the app is doing and for support.
  • Beta testing. If you test a beta version through TestFlight, Apple shares with us what TestFlight describes when you join, such as the name and email address used for your invitation, install and crash information, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve the app.

8. Games and content

FoldPlay includes no games, BIOS or firmware. You choose which game files to import, and you are responsible for having the right to use them. FoldPlay is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it does not collect any from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before or when that version is released. Continued use of a later version means the updated policy applies to it.
