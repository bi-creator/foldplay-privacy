FoldPlay

Dual-screen handheld games, built for foldable iPhone. For iPhone and iPad.

FoldPlay plays your own Nintendo DS game files. Fold your iPhone halfway and the game sits on the top screen while the touch screen and controls sit on the bottom, just like the original handheld.

What it does

  • Fold layout for foldable iPhone: game on one half, touch screen and controls on the other.
  • Portrait and landscape layouts on iPhone and iPad.
  • Switchable controller styles, plus 10 original themes inspired by game eras from 1980 to 1999.
  • Music while you play: keep your own music app playing, or build a soundtrack from Apple Music.
  • FoldPlay Originals: games made for FoldPlay, such as the street brawler Back Alley Brawl, as optional one-time purchases.
  • Quick save and load, plus named save states with a picture of each.
  • Import game files from the Files app, from another app, or by dropping them in FoldPlay's folder.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no data collected. Your games and saves stay on your device. Read the full privacy policy.

Games

Apart from the optional FoldPlay Originals, FoldPlay includes no games, BIOS or firmware. Import only game files you have the right to use, such as free homebrew games or your own backups.

Open source

FoldPlay is built on the open-source melonDS emulator and is released under the GNU GPL v3. The complete source code is on GitHub.
